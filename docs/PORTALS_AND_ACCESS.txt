BRILLANDA PORTALS & ACCESS ARCHITECTURE

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW & WHO USES IT
================================================================================

There are four kinds of people using Brillanda, and each one gets their own portal:

Portal        Who                            What They Do
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
School Admin  The principal or school office  Runs the term: sets up classes, adds
                                             teachers and students, watches scores
                                             come in, publishes results.

Teacher       Subject teachers               Enter scores for their own classes,
                                             then mark each class complete.

Parent        Parents or guardians           See their children's results and
                                             report cards.

Super Admin   Us, the Brillanda team         Look after all the schools on Brillanda.

Everyone uses the same website and the same sign-in page. After signing in, each
person lands in their own portal automatically.


================================================================================
2. HOW PEOPLE GET IN
================================================================================

Nobody signs themselves up. There is no "Create an account" button anywhere.
That's on purpose: a school's results are private, so every account is created
by someone above you.

- A school joins: The school fills in the "Request a trial" form on our website.
  We (the Brillanda team) look at it and create the school. The school's admin
  then gets an email invite.

- The admin invites teachers: The admin types a teacher's name and email, and
  the teacher gets an email invite.

- Parents get invited when their child is added: When the admin adds students
  (one by one or from a spreadsheet), each parent's email gets an invite.

- Parents with no email get a printed slip with an access code instead, and
  sign in with that code.

Rules and Details:
* An invite link works for 3 days (72 hours). The person clicks it, chooses a
  password, and they're in. If it runs out, they ask the school to send a new one.
* Signing in after that: email and password on the sign-in page. If someone
  forgets their password, the "Forgot password" link emails them a reset link.
* One email means one account across the platform.
* If a wrong password or an unknown email is typed, the message is the same
  either way ("That email and password don't match"). That stops strangers from
  guessing which emails are real.


================================================================================
3. WHAT EACH PORTAL DOES
================================================================================

SCHOOL ADMIN PORTAL
- Sees how far along the term is: how many subjects are in, which classes are
  ready, and who is behind.
- Nudges teachers who are behind, and says yes or no when a teacher asks to
  reopen a finished class.
- Publishes results for a class when all its subjects are done. This locks the
  scores and emails every parent.
- Manages classes, students, staff, the grading scale and term dates.

TEACHER PORTAL
- Sees only their own classes.
- Types in scores (first test, second test, exam). Totals and grades work
  themselves out as they type, and everything saves automatically.
- Clicks "Mark complete" when a class is finished. To change anything after that,
  they have to ask the admin to reopen it.

PARENT PORTAL
- Switches between their children.
- Sees each child's average, position in class, every subject's score, and the
  teachers' comments.
- Opens or downloads the report card.
- Gets an email the moment new results are published.


================================================================================
4. IS THERE A SUPER ADMIN PORTAL?
================================================================================

Yes, there is a Super Admin portal.

- Location in Codebase: apps/web/src/portals/super-admin
- Who uses it: The Brillanda internal team ("Us").
- What it does:
  * Looks after all schools registered on Brillanda.
  * Processes incoming trial requests submitted on the website.
  * Provisions new school accounts and initial admin credentials.
  * Monitors platform status, cross-school analytics, and tenant management.
